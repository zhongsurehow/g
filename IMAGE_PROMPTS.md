## 一、 道之牌 (The Path Cards)

### 1. 太极 (The Great Ultimate)
**核心意象:** 混沌、本源、无限潜力。
**提示词:**
`A swirling vortex of cosmic energy, black and white gases intermingling to form the Yin-Yang symbol (Taijitu). In the center of the symbol, a single, bright point of light pulses with potential. The background is a star-filled nebula. The scene evokes a sense of infinite possibility and primordial creation.`

### 2. 乾 (The Creative)
**核心意象:** 天、龙、创造力、阳刚。
**提示词:**
`A powerful, wise Chinese dragon, scales shimmering with celestial light, soaring through storm clouds above a vast landscape. Its claws grasp a glowing pearl of energy. The sky is a deep, imperial blue, and lightning flashes in the distance, illuminating the dragon's determined face. The scene feels majestic and full of creative power.`

### 3. 坤 (The Receptive)
**核心意象:** 地、大地母亲、包容、孕育。
**提示词:**
`A serene, vast landscape of rolling hills and fertile plains under a gentle, moonlit sky. In the center, the earth itself seems to form the shape of a sleeping, nurturing mother figure. A wide river flows peacefully through the land. The scene evokes a sense of peace, patience, and boundless receptivity.`

### 4. 坎 (The Abysmal)
**核心意象:** 深渊、水、危险、内在探索。
**提示词:**
`A lone traveler in a small boat navigating a dark, deep underground river. The water is dark but clear, revealing ancient, submerged ruins below. Strange, bioluminescent fish swim by. The only light comes from a single lantern on the boat, casting long shadows on the cavern walls. The mood is tense, mysterious, and introspective.`

### 5. 离 (The Clinging)
**核心意象:** 火、凤凰、光明、智慧。
**提示词:**
`A magnificent phoenix (Fenghuang) with fiery plumage, perched atop the roof of an ancient, ornate library pavilion. Its feathers glow with inner light, illuminating intricate scrolls and books visible through the windows. The background is a vibrant sunset. The scene represents clarity, wisdom, and cultural brilliance.`

### 6. 震 (The Arousing)
**核心意象:** 雷、觉醒、突如其来的变化。
**提示词:**
`A massive, ancient bell, covered in oracle bone script, is struck by a bolt of lightning from a dramatic, dark sky. The impact creates visible shockwaves of energy that ripple outwards, shaking the surrounding mountains and waking the sleeping animals. The scene is dynamic, powerful, and full of sudden, awakening energy.`

### 7. 巽 (The Gentle)
**核心意象:** 风、渗透、温和的影响。
**提示词:**
`A lone, elegant scholar stands on a mountain terrace, her long silk robes and hair gently flowing in the wind. The wind carries dandelion seeds and prayer flags across the valley. The influence is subtle but pervasive, shaping the clouds and bending the grass. The scene feels gentle, persistent, and influential.`

### 8. 艮 (The Keeping Still)
**核心意象:** 山、静止、沉思。
**提示词:**
`A colossal, ancient mountain peak shaped like a meditating sage. A solitary, gnarled pine tree clings to its cliffside. The world below is shrouded in a sea of clouds, creating a sense of profound stillness and isolation from the mundane world. The scene evokes deep meditation, stability, and inner peace.`

### 9. 兑 (The Joyous)
**核心意象:** 泽、喜悦、沟通、分享。
**提示词:**
`A beautiful, serene lake reflecting a clear autumn sky. On the shore, a group of friends dressed in elegant Hanfu are gathered in a pavilion, sharing tea, playing music (Guqin), and laughing. Koi fish swim joyfully in the lake. The scene is full of warmth, connection, and shared happiness.`

### 10. 中庸 (The Golden Mean)
**核心意象:** 平衡、和谐、中道。
**提示词:**
`A skilled artisan walking a tightrope between two towering cliffs, one of fiery lava (Yang) and one of cascading waterfalls (Yin). The artisan is perfectly balanced, holding a long pole with a glowing sphere at each end. The expression is calm and focused. The scene represents perfect balance, moderation, and finding harmony between opposites.`

### 11. 无为 (Wu Wei)
**核心意象:** 顺势而为、道法自然、轻松。
**提示词:**
`A Daoist sage is sleeping peacefully in a small boat, letting it drift down a gentle, winding river. The boat is guided by the natural current, effortlessly navigating around rocks. The surrounding landscape is a lush, untouched paradise, reminiscent of Zhangjiajie's peaks. The scene feels effortless, trusting, and in perfect harmony with the flow of nature.`

### 12. 轮回 (The Cycle)
**核心意象:** 循环、因果、重生。
**提示词:**
`A giant, ethereal water wheel, its sections depicting the four seasons in a continuous loop: a blooming spring, a lush summer, a golden autumn, and a snowy winter. The wheel turns slowly in a cosmic river of stars. At the top of the wheel, a new bud sprouts, symbolizing rebirth. The scene evokes the endless cycle of life, death, and rebirth.`

## 二、 五行牌 (The Five Elements Cards)

### 木 (Wood) Suit

**木之一 (Ace of Wood):** `A single, vibrant jade-green bamboo shoot, glowing with a soft inner light, powerfully breaking through the soil of a tranquil zen garden.`
**木之二 (Two of Wood):** `A wise scholar in Tang dynasty attire stands on a high palace balcony, holding a scroll, looking out at two diverging paths into a misty, ancient forest, contemplating a grand strategy.`
**木之三 (Three of Wood):** `Three majestic Chinese junk ships with large, billowing sails, sailing out from a bustling ancient harbor (like Quanzhou) towards a hopeful sunrise on the horizon.`
**木之四 (Four of Wood):** `A joyful celebration under a massive, blooming plum blossom tree in a courtyard. A harmonious family is gathered, sharing food and laughter, with ornate red lanterns hanging from the branches.`
**木之五 (Five of Wood):** `Five young martial artists, each with a different style of wooden staff, engaged in a dynamic but friendly sparring match within a dense bamboo forest. Their movements are a blur of energy.`
**木之六 (Six of Wood):** `A victorious general on a proud warhorse, his helmet adorned with laurel wreaths made of willow branches, parading through a gate of a cheering capital city as blossoms rain down.`
**木之七 (Seven of Wood):** `A single, determined warrior bravely defending a narrow mountain pass against seven shadowy figures below. He stands on higher ground, using a long wooden spear, embodying courage.`
**木之八 (Eight of Wood):** `Eight polished wooden arrows, fletched with elegant crane feathers, flying swiftly and in perfect formation across a dawn sky towards a distant, unseen target.`
**木之九 (Nine of Wood):** `An old, weary guardian leaning on his heavy staff, standing before a thick, thorny wooden gate. He is wounded but his gaze is unbroken and vigilant, representing resilience.`
**木之十 (Ten of Wood):** `A single person struggling to carry a huge bundle of ten heavy logs on their back, walking up a steep, seemingly endless mountain path, their body bent under the immense pressure.`

**火之六 (Six of Fire):** `A triumphant hero, holding a tall banner with a fire phoenix emblem, being carried on the shoulders of his cheering, joyous comrades through a crowd of admirers.`

**火之十 (Ten of Fire):** `A figure almost completely obscured by the crushing weight of ten crossed, heavy flaming torches they are attempting to carry, symbolizing burnout.`

**木之相 (Minister of Wood):** `A confident and charismatic female minister, seated on a throne carved from a living, flowering tree. She holds a budding staff, and her presence is so vibrant that flowers bloom around her feet. She exudes an aura of creative leadership.`
**木之帝 (Emperor of Wood):** `A visionary emperor with a wise and determined gaze, standing before a large, detailed map of his expanding empire. He holds a scepter that looks like a miniature, perfectly formed bonsai tree, symbolizing his role as a master planner and creator.`
